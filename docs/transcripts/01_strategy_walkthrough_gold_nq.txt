CORE METHODOLOGY (HIS OWN WORDS, CLEANED)
================================================================

OPENING — Indicator overview:
Working on a weekly chart. Indicators on screen: Supply and Demand by
LuxAlgo (free), Key Levels (free), ICT Kill Zones (free), Spaceman Key
Levels (free). Volume indicator at bottom is Arena by DGT — shows
strength of buying vs selling with a percentage box. Visually pleasing
but essentially tracking buy/sell strength on the candles.

TRADER IDENTITY:
"I'm a trader where I wait for breaks and trends, I wait for pivots,
I wait for supply and demand, or you can trade our daily high retest
breaks. My main go-to strategy would be waiting for a daily high or
daily open — we would retest that and then distribute after that."

PRE-TRADE ROUTINE:
1. Coffee first.
2. Check the news — Discord community has Futures Trading news links,
   Forex.com economic calendars, and earnings calendar links.
3. Focus on Gold (primary) and NQ (secondary). Used to trade platinum
   and crude oil, narrowed down because more than 2 assets created
   stress and noise.
4. For Gold: also watch the Dollar (DXY) and other precious metals.
5. For NQ: watch NVDA, SPY, VIX, Magnificent 7, big tech stocks.

DAILY CHART APPROACH:
- Key levels are pre-marked by the indicator — doesn't manually draw.
- Watches how price reacts at each key level.
- When price reaches a level with "money resting" or "order blocks"
  or "liquidity," he calls it "market chop" — staying between levels.
- Price either bounces between levels or breaks to a new range.

CORE PHILOSOPHY:
"My methodology and psychology on trading is so simple. If the volume's
there, it's going to move. If the volume's not there, it's not going
to move. All we have to do is be on the right side of the move when
the move is happening."

ON OTHER INDICATORS:
"I've tried every other indicator there is in the book. Almost every
indicator is lagged. So does that really help us when we need current
price action?"

ORDER DOM USAGE:
"The only thing I would be looking for at the DOM is — there's usually
numbers to the low end and to the high end with higher volume. I see
what numbers keep being bounced back and forth with higher volume,
aligned with the charts, and see where supply is being generated or
where demand is going to stop."

GOLD TRADE EXAMPLE (LIVE):
- Price came back to a major daily key level.
- His favorite entry points: daily open, daily high, daily low.
- Identified that gold reached the monthly high — possible loss of
  momentum.
- All he needs to enter: "Volume — I need volume to enter near any
  sort of key level."

4-HOUR APPROACH:
Same concept as other timeframes. Looking for bounces/breaks at key
levels with volume. The 4H showed Gold broke daily low and monthly
open price, then bounced at the daily low key level.

ON GAPS / FAIR VALUE GAPS:
"Some might call it missed entries, liquidity resting, or orders that
might be there. I just call it a gap because I don't need to call it
anything else."

ON MARKET MOVEMENT:
"The only reason any of this is ever moving is human sentiment. Someone
somewhere is pressing a buy and sell button. It could be us degen
retail traders, or it could be your big banks, institutions, groups
doing this in calculated algorithmic moves. They know retail traders
sit there trying to come up with all these terms when realistically
price is reacting to human sentiment."

ADVICE FOR BEGINNERS:
"Focus on how news affects the market. How do people spend money
during turmoil, recession, high inflation. That gives you an edge on
how money flows."

SESSION TIMING (IMPORTANT):
"When trading gold, through the day, New York open to close, whatever
trend or way the money was going during those sessions will always
follow suit around the Asian market time around 9:15 to 9:35 to 9:45.
Every time between 9:15 and 9:40, the market session will follow the
trend within the entirety of the New York session. I found high
accuracy just trading that alone."

1-HOUR APPROACH:
"Lately I've been getting entries on the 1-hour timeframe alone. I
like to get entries on the 5-minute but we're not there yet."

ON CONFLUENCE / CHANGING BIAS:
"On the 4H my bias was longs. On the 1H it changed because price came
to a daily high, touched it twice, quickly fell and rejected. Sellers
came in, buyers tried to fight back at another key level (an AVWAP
level). Buyers tried to stop sellers — retail traders probably thought
'oh it's an FVG, it's a retest, let's buy.' Dumb money bought there
because they saw an FVG form on the 1H. Scalpers probably made money,
but average gamblers who hold trades got [stopped out]."

15-MIN AND 5-MIN APPROACH:
"The 5-minute is where I use my entries. This is my entry point. All
I need to do is be near a key level — daily levels are my favorite
zones."

ON SMALLER TIMEFRAME NOISE:
"On the 5 minute it would be our demand. Price doesn't really know
where it wants to go. We had a spike, probably had volume, what time
was this — yes there was volume that came in. Probably power hour or
something. It quickly died off."

DAILY P&L TARGETS:
"My strategy / system is I like to target between $2,000 and $5,000
a day depending on market conditions. If my setups are not showing up
and my first trade was rough, my confidence will be different — I'll
lower my target based on confidence. When I'm in wins, mindset is
different, I raise my target."

ON RISK:
"You shouldn't be taking any unnecessary risks until you can afford
to say 'I can sit here and lose $1,000 and it's not going to affect
me.' When you get to that level, that's when you can take more risks."

PSYCHOLOGY OVER CHARTING:
"For me, charting does not outweigh the psychology of trading. Now
that you've seen on my charts how price reacts at certain key levels,
it's: do I have the confidence to enter when I first see that switch?
Do I have that anticipation? What is going on in my mind?"
