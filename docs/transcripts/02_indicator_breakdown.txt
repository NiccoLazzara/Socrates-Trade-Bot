#1 — KEY LEVELS by Trading Wolf
- Search term in TradingView: "key levels"
- Use: First indicator that pops up by Trading Wolf
- Function: Marks daily open, daily low, AVWAP levels, moving averages
- Why: "Visually pleasing" + auto-marks levels he would otherwise draw
  by hand. Removes manual work.

#2 — KEY LEVELS by Spaceman
- Search term: "key levels" (second result)
- Use: Alternative/complementary key levels indicator
- Function: Visual key levels (slightly different display style)
- Why: Both Spaceman and Trading Wolf are visually pleasing. Uses both.

KEY DIFFERENCE between the two:
"Trading Wolf will add your daily open and your daily low, and it will
add your AVWAP levels, your average moving averages — so if you like
to look at moving averages it could be useful."

#3 — BULL vs BEAR by Arena (DGT)
- Search term: "bull verse bear"
- Use: First result that pops up
- Function: Volume strength indicator showing bull-side vs bear-side
  strength with percentages
- Why: "Just showing me the bull side and the bear side strength. It's
  visually pleasing. I like to see the percentages — I don't know why
  but it'll tell you the trend. If you like your ADX and all that other
  jazz, good for you, I don't really use it. But I like to see the
  flips and the volume and the switches and the velocity and how it's
  moving."

#4 — ORDER DOM (TradingView native)
- Not a Pine Script — native TradingView feature
- Use: Aligned with chart analysis to see where volume is being tagged
- Function: Shows live volume at each price level
- Why: Identifies where supply is being generated or where demand will
  stop

#5 — ICT KILL ZONES by LuxAlgo
- Search term: "ICT kill zones"
- Use: First result, by LuxAlgo
- Function: Highlights Asian, London, and New York session windows
- Why: "I like to know where key levels are — a key level for me would
  be where New York opened and closed, where London opened and closed."
- Methodology: Reviews each session's behavior — did Asian session
  range or break out? Did London range or trend? Uses this to inform
  the NY session bias.

#6 — SUPPLY AND DEMAND (Visible Range) by LuxAlgo
- Search term: "supply and demand"
- Use: First result, "Supply and Demand Visible Range by LuxAlgo"
- Function: Auto-plots supply and demand zones in the visible range
- Why: "Visually shows you the supply and the demand where you could
  also take potential entries and exits."
