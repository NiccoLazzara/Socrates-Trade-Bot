CORE PIVOT METHODOLOGY (HIS OWN WORDS, CLEANED)
================================================================

CONTEXT — TRADE TAKEN EARLIER:
"We took one trade today in the NASDAQ before. It was a nice win. We
took a bounce off our lower demand. We noticed we weren't coming any
lower, and look where I took my exit — I took it at the previous key
level, our previous area where we pivoted."

INDICATOR STACK CONFIRMED (SAME AS PRIOR VIDEOS):
- Key Levels indicator (free)
- Supply and Demand
- Volume: Bull vs Bear by Arena (DGT)

PIVOT DEFINITION (CRITICAL FOR BUILD):
A pivot is "previous areas on higher time frames where price either
broke above or broke below or changed direction. Could be for a
second, could be for a minute, but that's all I need."

→ This is a key build rule: PIVOTS = previous price action turning
  points visible on higher timeframes. NOT the floor-trader pivot
  calculation. He uses the term loosely to mean structural pivots.

ENTRY LOGIC (NQ EXAMPLE):
"Let's say we entered long here at this previous week low — which is
a previous area where we kind of pivoted (look on previous price
history). I would need volume, and I would look to take my exits
based on these pivots."

EXIT LOGIC:
"One of my TPs we could mark out a line right here — this would be a
potential area for me to take profits based on these areas where
price changed direction for a minute, and that's it."

ON CONTINUATION:
"A lot of people probably say 'how do you know when to continue to
hold?' That comes into hand with how much money are you looking to
make. Are you a scalper? What I would look for to continue these
breakouts would only be volume. My only thoughts when we approach
other key levels: are we going to continue higher, are we going to
retest? There's nothing really else to think about. If the volume is
following suit, then we can play the game."

================================================================
MARKET STRUCTURE READING
================================================================

TREND STRUCTURE:
"When we're in trends, we'll create higher lows, higher lows, higher
lows, until price will reach some sort of previous area of demand."

RANGE STRUCTURE:
"On the 1 hour we're in this range from our lower demand to mid zone,
and we've kind of been not creating any other areas until we fully
broke out and touched our previous area of supply."

DOUBLE TOP RECOGNITION:
"We have a double top on a 1-hour timeframe. People — based on this,
we're not creating any downfalls. This is a key area right here. Based
on economic news, we might want to come all the way back down to these
previous pivots."

REJECTION READ:
"Look what happened when we reached an previous area of upper supply
— last time we reached this area, it rejected heavy. So what does
that tell me in my mind?"

DAY-OF-WEEK FILTER (IMPORTANT)
================================================================
"Today is Friday when I'm making this video. On Friday you really
won't get the best price actions. You can make some good trades on
Friday but keep in mind your system won't work every day. When I see
movement like this on a higher time frame, it kind of generates some

→ BUILD RULE: Friday afternoon should be down-weighted or filtered

================================================================
PSYCHOLOGY OF PIVOT TRADING
================================================================

"Anytime we're near a pivot, we can't have this anticipation. With my
style I'm either right or I'm wrong. Are we at a key level and is
there volume? That's the only thing I need."

"I'm using pattern history, price action history, with current price
action by key levels, supply and demand, with volume. That's pretty
much all I need."

ON CHOP DAYS:
"In days where we're chopping in between, I really don't have any
trades. I would be looking for: are we bouncing through key levels
with volume? That is it."

================================================================
KEY EXTRACTABLE RULES
================================================================
1. PIVOT DEFINITION: Previous price action turning points on higher
   timeframes — NOT floor-trader pivot calculation
2. ENTRY = Pivot + Volume confirmation
3. EXIT = Next pivot (where price previously changed direction)
4. CONTINUATION CHECK = Volume at the next key level
5. STRUCTURE READING:
   - Trend = higher lows / lower highs sequence
   - Range = bouncing between supply and demand
   - Double top/bottom = exhaustion signal
   - Heavy rejection at level = strong S/R going forward
6. FRIDAY FILTER: Down-weight Friday afternoon alerts (his stated
   low-edge period)
7. CHOP DAYS: No setup until clean break of a key level with volume
